David earned his Ph.D. in [Genomics and Computational Biology](https://www.med.upenn.edu/gcb/) at the University of Pennsylvania, where he used deep learning and natural language processing to analyze biomedical text.
He joined the [Greenelab](https://greenelab.com) in 2017 and was appointed as a trainee on the T32 Computational Genomics training grant from the National Human Genome Research Institute (NHGRI).
Before Penn, he graduated from the University of Maryland Baltimore County (UMBC) with a B.S. in Computer Science and a minor in Bioinformatics.
During his time at UMBC, he participated in the Meyerhoff Scholars program (M23) and was appointed as a Marc U*Star Scholar.
After defending, he became a Data Scientist at Digital Science & Research Solutions, where he works on projects that help government funders understand research trends and topics.
